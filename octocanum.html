04 Octocanum Drivetrain

High school · FIRST Tech Challenge Team 12791 Iterative Intentions · POWERPLAY season, 2022 to 2023

A parallel-plate mecanum drivetrain with four deployable traction wheels, so the robot could switch between holonomic and tank drive mid-match. Mecanum gives agility and omnidirectional movement; the traction wheels give grip and acceleration when pushing or driving straight under load. The CAD is on GrabCAD.
